---
title: What does it take to connect to a macos host with mosh
---
mosh 使用者一直以來在某個版本的 application firewall 升級後就沒辦法有可靠的方法能夠連上 mac 伺服器。有些人辦得到有些人怎麼都弄不出來，以下是成功案例。
```
sudo /usr/libexec/ApplicationFirewall/socketfilterfw --setglobalstate off
sudo /usr/libexec/ApplicationFirewall/socketfilterfw --add $(which mosh-server)
sudo /usr/libexec/ApplicationFirewall/socketfilterfw --unblockapp $(which mosh-server)
sudo /usr/libexec/ApplicationFirewall/socketfilterfw --setglobalstate on
```
via: [OS X: Nothing received from server on UDP port 60003](https://github.com/mobile-shell/mosh/issues/1254#issuecomment-4308942403)
