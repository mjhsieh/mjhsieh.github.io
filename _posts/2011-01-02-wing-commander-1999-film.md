 I totally agree with all the critics about this movie. It has the worst story I’ve even seen from a movie. However it’s not totally bullshit. The character settings, special effects, and acting performance were all above okay-levels. You can find out a lot of 21st-century sci-fi dramas are reusing those elements from “Wing Commander”.
