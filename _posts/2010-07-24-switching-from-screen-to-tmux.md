1. It somehow shields you from Terminal.app crash when you are typing a lot via “Input methods”.
2. It is faster. (this is probably not true, my benchmark “time cat rfc3261.txt” tells me that screen is faster.
3. It is heavily, actively developed.
4. it’s BSD-licensed, duh…
